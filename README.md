## Professional Summary

I am building my career in Cloud and DevOps with a strong focus on AWS, Infrastructure as Code, CI/CD, containers, observability, security and operational reliability.

My background includes more than 10 years in HSE and critical mining operations. That experience gives me a practical operational mindset: risk management, incident response, compliance, documentation, preventive controls and continuous improvement. I now apply that mindset to cloud infrastructure and DevOps workflows.

> My goal is to contribute in Cloud Engineer, DevOps Junior/Mid, Cloud Operations, Platform Engineering or SRE Junior roles.

## What I Build

- Reproducible AWS infrastructure with Terraform and CloudFormation.
- CI/CD validation with GitHub Actions.
- Containerized workloads with Docker, ECS and Kubernetes/EKS.
- Observability stacks with Prometheus, Grafana, Loki and CloudWatch.
- Serverless automation with Lambda and EventBridge.
- Operational documentation: runbooks, incident response and security checklists.
